Tenant copy scoping migration analysis, 2026-11-09.
The existing unscoped copy event marks do not encode a tenant. A direct switch to tenant-qualified event keys would not match old marks. During rolling deploy, old and new code would not arbitrate on the same key; treating old marks as applying to every tenant avoids some replay but retains suppression. Additional ownership and intent identifiers also derive from unscoped source trade data; position models have no tenant identifier. Recovery seed ownership and durable tenant-scoped history must be validated before changing identity or claiming tenant isolation. Source inspection only; no migration implemented.

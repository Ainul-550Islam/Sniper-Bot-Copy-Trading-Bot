# A stale replica now posts a sell at the COMMITTED cost (2026-11-09)

Closes the only `#[ignore]` left in `crates/` and the row that was parked as
"OPEN — defect REPRODUCED by a real witness; fix pending".

## The defect

`GlobalLedger::submit` priced a sell with `inner.book.cost_of_slice(&event)` — the LOCAL
book of that replica. Replica B never saw the opening buy, so its book had no position and
`cost_of_slice` returned 0. The sell was then journaled with:

  inventory credit  = 0        (nothing removed at cost)
  realized PnL      = proceeds (the whole sale counted as profit)

The witness (`accounting_replica_order::stale_replica_sell_posting_uses_committed_inventory_cost`)
ran with no database at all and failed in 0.00 s:
  "a shared-journal sell must credit the committed 0.5 SOL cost, got -0"

## The fix — price from what the journal COMMITTED

1. `GlobalLedger::committed_slice_cost(...)`: if this replica has applied nothing for the
   position, or the journal holds history older than what this replica knows, the slice is
   priced from the journal instead of the local book. Cost-free when the local book already
   covers the position's history (the replay reproduces it).
2. `GlobalLedger::journal_slice_cost(...)`: replays the position's committed events in
   journal order and applies them from their RECORDED POSTINGS
   (`PositionBook::apply_committed`) rather than recomputing a local cost — that is what
   makes it correct on a replica that never saw the buy.
3. `PositionBook::apply_committed(event, id, &Entry)`: inventory movement and realized
   result come FROM the committed postings (`inventory` credit = cost of the slice).
   Cash-only events delegate to `apply` — they are fully determined by the event.
4. `LedgerStore::load_entry(&event_id) -> Option<Option<Entry>>`: new trait method with a
   conservative default (`None` = cannot answer). Implemented for `MemoryLedgerStore` and,
   over `ledger_postings`, for the server's `DbLedgerStore`
   (`AccountingRepo::load_entry`, `ORDER BY seq`, unknown account/side or negative seq
   decoded as a hard error).
5. `submit` and `hydrate` both use it, so the live path and the replay path agree.

Fail-closed by construction: if the journal is unavailable, a committed posting cannot be
decoded, or a history row is invalid, the LOCAL cost is used and a `warn!` names the event.
A missing cost is never silently zero — it is either committed or reported.

No schema change: `ledger_postings` already had the committed amounts.

## What this does not establish

* The replay is O(history of that position) per sell when a replica is behind. That is the
  price of correctness here; a materialised committed-cost column would be the perf
  follow-up, and it would need a migration this change deliberately avoided.
* `load_entry` has a conservative default, so a third-party `LedgerStore` implementation
  that does not implement it keeps the OLD (local-book) behaviour rather than failing —
  visible as the `warn!`, not silent. Both in-repo stores implement it.
* Only the cost of a sell is taken from the journal. Other cross-replica divergences
  (fee attribution, reconciliation aggregation scope) remain separate ledger rows.

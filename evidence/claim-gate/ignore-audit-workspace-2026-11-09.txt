## The one that stayed, and why
crates/core/tests/accounting_replica_order.rs
  #[ignore = "open multi-replica accounting defect; run explicitly while designing a fix"]
  stale_replica_sell_posting_uses_committed_inventory_cost  ->  FAILED in 0.00s
  panicked at accounting_replica_order.rs:42:5
    a shared-journal sell must credit the committed 0.5 SOL cost, got -0

This test needs NO database and NO network: two GlobalLedger replicas share one
MemoryLedgerStore, A books a 10.0 SOL buy, B books a sell of half. The sell must be
posted against the cost basis committed to the shared journal by A; it is posted
against nothing (-0), because each replica calculates the posting from its own local
book before writing to the shared journal.

It is deliberately left ignored. The three options and why they are wrong:
  (a) leave the assertion, remove #[ignore]  -> the whole workspace sweep goes red for
      a defect nobody has fixed yet, and a red sweep stops being an alarm.
  (b) leave it ignored and say nothing       -> what the gap table did before today;
      this file is the correction.
  (c) weaken the assertion so it passes      -> destroys the only witness of the bug.
So: witness kept, defect kept OPEN, and the row in GAP-MISSING-FILES.md upgraded from
'source observation' to 'defect REPRODUCED by a real witness; fix pending'.

## What is actually required to close it (not done here)
The posting must be derived from committed journal state, not from replica-local book
state: either the shared insert must compute cost from the journal (a single SQL
statement that reads committed inventory) or a replica's sell must be refused/re-priced
when its local book disagrees with the journal. That is a design change in the ledger
write path, not a one-line fix, and it needs its own RED/GREEN cycle.
